Research ( / )

I work on physics-informed 3D reconstruction. Methods such as 3D Gaussian Splatting reproduce how a scene looks, but not how it moves or what it is made of. My work estimates physical properties from observations and uses simulation to keep a reconstruction physically consistent: recovering an object's friction and velocity so it stays in the scene while no camera can see it, or recovering the material parameters behind a simulated orange peel. This work forms my MASc thesis, Physics-Informed Dynamic 3D Reconstruction: From Gaussian Splatting and Rigid-Body Simulation to Inverse Material Estimation.
