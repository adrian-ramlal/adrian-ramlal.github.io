Research

My research is on physics-informed 3D reconstruction. I estimate physical parameters, such as friction, initial velocity and material properties, from observed behaviour, and use differentiable or learned simulators to keep a reconstruction consistent with physics, for example while an object is hidden from every camera. My MASc thesis is Physics-Informed Dynamic 3D Reconstruction: From Gaussian Splatting and Rigid-Body Simulation to Inverse Material Estimation. Representative papers are highlighted.
